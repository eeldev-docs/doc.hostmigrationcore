Features

Snapshot traffic

Snapshots are compressed and sent in chunks over reliable RPCs.

Delta snapshots​

Each snapshot gets a sequence number. A client that has the previous one only gets a delta: the records (an actor, a player, the globals) that changed, plus removals. New clients get a full snapshot. An unchanged world sends nothing.

Every delta carries a hash of the complete state. If a client's rebuilt snapshot does not match, or a delta does not follow the snapshot it has, the client asks the host for a full snapshot. This happens by itself.

Measured with 4 players, 300 static props and gameplay going on:

Size
Full snapshot27 KB
Deltaabout 0.9 KB
Host traffic with deltasabout 4 KB/s
Host traffic without deltasabout 40 KB/s

Tuning​

  • Snapshot Interval: lower means less progress lost on a crash, but more traffic.
  • Use Delta Snapshots: turn off to always send full snapshots.
  • Chunk Size and Max Snapshot Bytes: chunk size per RPC, and the size above which a snapshot is dropped with a warning.
  • Get Snapshot Stats (host) returns full size, last delta size, changed records, bytes per second and counts of fulls, deltas and resyncs. A summary is logged every 30 s.
  • Only mark what must survive as SaveGame. Large records that change often (big inventories) are resent whole every time they change.

Testing resyncs​

HostMigration.Debug.DropEveryNthDelta N on a client drops every Nth delta, to see the resync at work.
